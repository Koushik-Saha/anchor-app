Anchor

Privacy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Anchor collects no data. There is no account, no sign-up, no analytics and no tracking. Everything you do in Anchor stays on your device. We can't see it, because it never reaches us.

What we collect

Nothing. Anchor:

  • has no accounts, logins or sign-ups;
  • has no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting tools;
  • sets no cookies;
  • has no in-app purchases: every feature is free;
  • never connects to the internet itself, and works fully in airplane mode.

What stays on your device

To work, Anchor saves a few things in your phone's or browser's own storage. Only you can see them:

  • Settings: haptics, sound, tone pitch, text size and motion.
  • History: the date and time of each reset, which one you chose, whether you finished it, and your answer to “What helped?”.
  • My plan, if you write one: your note to yourself, an emergency contact’s name and number, and a private note.

You can remove it any time: History → Delete all history, My plan → Delete my plan, or uninstall Anchor (on the web: clear this site’s data). Then it’s gone.

If you back up your phone (for example with iCloud or Google), that backup may include Anchor’s data, as it does for other apps. It goes to your own account, never to us.

Calls and texts

The “Call or text 988” links and the button for your emergency contact simply open your phone’s own calling or messaging app. Anchor isn’t involved in the call and keeps no record of it.

The website

The web version is a set of files that run in your browser, with no cookies and no analytics. Like any website, the server that delivers those files may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP address and browser type) to keep it running safely. We don’t use them to identify you or combine them with anything.

Children

Anchor collects no data from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Anchor is a self-help tool. It is not a medical device and not a substitute for professional care. If you are in crisis in the US, call or text 988. Elsewhere, contact your local emergency number.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, we will update this page and the date above. Anchor will never start collecting data without saying so clearly first.
